# LINEHORSE — Construction Intelligence

**BUILT FOR BUILDERS, BY BUILDERS.**

“Capture construction reality once. Turn it into useful intelligence everywhere. Preserve it for the life of the property.”

LINEHORSE is a builder-first digital toolbag over a construction operating system. The builder provides construction intelligence; SITEPM provides digital leverage. It scales from the person building, to the person running a job, to the person running the company. Models are replaceable. Structured records, evidence, permissions, workflows, integrations, and historical memory are durable assets.

The interface is a superintendent's working desk: professional, rugged, precise, restrained, and practical. AI enhances verified operational information; basic construction workflows must work without it. Planned work, assigned resources, actual attendance, completed work, and evidence remain distinct.

The canonical detailed architecture remains [docs/PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE.md). This summary does not authorize additional product lines or Ask Stage 4. Sprint 4's explicit scheduling authorization adds structured planning and resource coordination to that foundation.

The initial commercial objective is to earn the trust of **10 paying contractors using LINEHORSE on real projects**. Prioritize usefulness, reliability, retained project history, and customer trust over feature count.
