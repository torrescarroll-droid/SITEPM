# LINEHORSE — Construction Intelligence

**BUILT FOR BUILDERS, BY BUILDERS.**

“Capture construction reality once. Turn it into useful intelligence everywhere. Preserve it for the life of the property.”

LINEHORSE is a builder-first digital toolbag over a construction operating system. The builder provides construction intelligence; SITEPM provides digital leverage. It scales from the person building, to the person running a job, to the person running the company. Models are replaceable. Structured records, evidence, permissions, workflows, integrations, and historical memory are durable assets.

The interface is a superintendent's working desk: professional, rugged, precise, restrained, and practical. AI enhances verified operational information; basic construction workflows must work without it. Planned work, assigned resources, actual attendance, completed work, and evidence remain distinct.

The canonical detailed architecture remains [docs/PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE.md). This summary does not authorize additional product lines or Ask Stage 4. Sprint 4's explicit scheduling authorization adds structured planning and resource coordination to that foundation.

The initial commercial objective is to earn the trust of **10 paying contractors using LINEHORSE on real projects**. Prioritize usefulness, reliability, retained project history, and customer trust over feature count.

Sprint 5’s explicit authorization adds retained, immutable project document versions and useful retrieval to the existing evidence foundation. User-selected current issues, original bytes, source hashes, page citations and operational references preserve provenance. Extracted source text and AI interpretations remain distinct; uploading or promoting a document does not authorize downstream construction, schedule, financial or approval actions. This authorization does not start unrelated product lines.

Sprint 6 brand direction: **LINEHORSE — KEEP YOUR PROJECT RUNNING.** Construction Intelligence remains the positioning and BUILT FOR BUILDERS, BY BUILDERS remains foundational. The practical working-desk principle supports the existing multi-role company workspace; it does not restrict the product to superintendents or invent new roles/permissions. The supplied visual reference guides reusable presentation, not feature or architectural expansion. The horse logo remains exploratory and is not an approved asset.


## 2026-10-09 — Preserved product direction

**Power underneath. Clarity on the surface. Clarity at Every Level.** One permissioned construction record supports field, superintendent, PM, operations/executive, finance and future client responsibilities. Personalization is not authorization. The working-desk design is a practical lens within a multi-role platform; retain the Sprint 6 charcoal/copper/warm-neutral system and refine it through real use.

Reported work, reviewer-verified physical progress, earned contract value, cost, billed amounts and cash are distinct. Future scope-weighted progress and monthly pay applications must preserve evidence, approved baseline/change history and human authority. Shared Project Health and a restricted client workspace should reuse common definitions while protecting internal prices, margins, rates and drafts. These are recorded requirements, not current capabilities.

Selective offline field operation is future direction: work anywhere, synchronize when connected, with explicit local/pending/conflict/server-confirmed states. Current browser drafts and resumable uploads are foundations, not whole-app offline support. Master scheduling and document federation remain discovery-led extensions, not claims of current Gantt/CPM/connectors.

The [permanent product record](docs/product/README.md) preserves requirements, feature status, decision history, discoveries and unresolved choices. The [dated roadmap](ROADMAP.md#2026-10-09--product-knowledge-audit-current-status-and-proposed-horizons) distinguishes implementation, verification and release.
